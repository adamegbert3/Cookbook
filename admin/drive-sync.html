☁️ Google Drive Sync

For family members without internet access. Mirrors every visible recipe into a shared Google Drive folder as Google Docs. When you save, approve, or delete a recipe from this admin console while local-tools/scan-recipe/serve-locally.mjs is running, this now kicks off automatically in the background — no need to remember to run it. As a backup, it also runs on its own schedule (see local-tools/sync-to-drive/README.md) so changes made from your phone still make it to Drive within a few hours even if that helper isn't open.
